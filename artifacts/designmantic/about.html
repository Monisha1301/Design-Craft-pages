Our story

We help small businesses look like big brands

DesignCraft Studio started in 2021 with a simple idea: great branding should not need an agency budget or a month of waiting.

Built by designers and engineers

What began as a small logo generator for local cafes in Coimbatore has grown into a full creative suite used by founders, freelancers and agencies in 96 countries.

Every template is hand-crafted by our design team and every tool is built in-house, so we can keep improving the details that matter: sharp vectors, accurate colours and reliable print quality.
